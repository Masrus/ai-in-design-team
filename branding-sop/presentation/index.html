FAQ

What is a white-label trading platform?

A white-label trading platform is ready-made trading software that a broker or prop firm launches under its own brand. With cTrader, your name and logo replace the cTrader brand across web, desktop and mobile apps, the admin back office and client emails.

Which apps and screens carry my brand?

Favicon, desktop and dock icons, installer, notifications and login screens on Web and Desktop; app icon, splash screen, login and menu on iOS and Android; your App Store and Google Play listings; cTrader Admin; and transactional emails in light and dark themes.

Is the white-label platform suitable for prop firms?

Yes. Prop firms get the same branded web, desktop and mobile apps, admin tools and emails as brokers, all under their own name.

Will my mobile apps be published under my company name?

Yes. Your iOS and Android apps are listed in the App Store and Google Play under your name and with your icon.

What logo files do I need?

An SVG or PNG logo with a transparent background. You can add a separate version for dark backgrounds. Your app icon is cropped from the logo.

Do I need to migrate my existing clients?

No. You can add cTrader as a branded trading platform without migrating your existing client base.
